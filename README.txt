index, move it to the table center, and hold still for two seconds. No left
pinch is needed. Place → Reposition reveals arrows around the paper. Move
left/right/near/away by 1 cm, height by 2 mm, and rotate by 5 degrees. Done
restores the menus. Choose a New Table Spot restarts fingertip calibration.

then replay the take. A life-size textured Microsoft Rocketbox human mirrors
those recorded movements across the table. Body/arm poses are reconstructed;
tracking gaps hide the character. It is not photorealistic full-body capture.
